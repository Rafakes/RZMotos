## Rider animations and code (2.0.0)

All rider animations are generated by this mod's own offline tool from Project Zomboid's vanilla
animations (Bob_IdleDriving and Bob_Idle): seat, grips, foot pegs and steering axis are measured on
this mod's own motorcycle models. Animation sets are generated from the vanilla player-vehicle
animation sets. All Lua code is original to this mod. No third-party mod assets or code are included.

## Street Three UV and diffuse revision (1.5.0)

Street Three and Street Three Matte now use a shared semantic UV layout with 16 part regions and individual 4096 diffuse atlases. Source material identities were transferred from the original Three Cylinder Naked Street Bike model. Native Blender procedural surface grain, soft ambient occlusion and diffuse shading replace the earlier fragmented color/swatch bake for these two variants. Geometry and mechanical pivots are retained. Editable sources, mapping guides and build steps are preserved in `design/uv-rework/`. Original attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms above continue to apply.
