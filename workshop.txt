description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Dedicated rider animations per bike: riding, foot down when stopped, reverse, arms on the handlebar, mounting and dismounting
description=[*]Animated wheels, discs and front end, removable parts and storage, and its own mechanics screen
description=[*]102 craftable motorcycle parts under the Tuning category, with English and Brazilian Portuguese translations
description=[*]Police siren and flashing blue/red lights on the Electra Glide (radial menu, [b]V[/b] key)
description=[*]Rechargeable battery on the electric scooter
description=[*]A hard crash throws the rider off; a fallen bike can be stood back up with the "Right the motorcycle" option
description=[*]Tuk-tuk handled as a car, with a central driver, two passengers and vanilla occupant animations
description=[*]Trailer that loads and unloads nearby motorcycles from the context menu
description=[*]Detailed 4K matte textures, world spawns and parts in loot
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Crafting[/h2]
description=Parts are made in the Tuning category, with recipes locked. The [b]RefugeZ Motorcycle Maintenance Manual[/b] teaches all 102; three levels above the required skill a character learns them anyway.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Seats and tires[/b] (worn, standard, performance): Mechanics 2 - converted from the vanilla part, which keeps its condition
description=[*][b]Windscreens[/b]: Mechanics 1
description=[*][b]Fenders, top cases, panniers and cargo racks[/b]: Welding 4, with a blowtorch, welding mask, sheet metal and screws
description=[*][b]Fuel tanks[/b]: Welding 5
description=[*][b]Old West side holster[/b]: Tailoring 3 - [b]Post-Apo cargo pack[/b]: Tailoring 4, with leather strips, thread, a needle and scissors
description=[*][b]Delivery scooter traction battery[/b]: Electrical 3 and Mechanics 2, consuming a whole car battery, which keeps its charge
description=[/list]
description=The manual spawns in garages, auto shops, bookstores and in the bikes' top cases.
